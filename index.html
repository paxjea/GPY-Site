Who We Are

About George Parker Young

Board Certified in Civil Trial Law · Member, American Board of Trial Advocates (ABOTA)

Four decades in the arena.

George Parker Young is a trial and appellate lawyer with more than 40 years in complex litigation — business, tort, oil and gas, fiduciary duty, corporate governance, cybersecurity, and intellectual property. He has tried and argued cases from Texas district courts to the U.S. Supreme Court, and has handled 25+ appeals along the way.

His work spans high-profile class actions, multidistrict litigation, and minority-shareholder-oppression cases, on which he has written and lectured extensively. Board Certified in Civil Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization, he is consistently recognized as a Texas Super Lawyer and a Best Lawyer in America.

Notable Achievements
  • $52.5M settlement for 13,000 royalty owners — Barnett Shale MDL.
  • Lead appellate counsel, Bell v. Chesapeake Energy — a landmark lease-interpretation win for royalty owners.
  • Argued an HMO-liability case before the U.S. Supreme Court (CIGNA v. Calad).
  • First Texas verdict under the 1997 HMO Liability Act — a judgment exceeding $13 million.
  • Finalist, Trial Lawyer of the Year (2003), Trial Lawyers for Public Justice.
Affiliations · Teaching · Publications
  • Litigation Council, State Bar of Texas — six years.
  • Adjunct Professor, Texas Wesleyan School of Law (now Texas A&M).
  • Co-Course Director, multiple State Bar of Texas CLE programs.
  • Author and frequent speaker on fiduciary duty, shareholder oppression, and HMO liability.
